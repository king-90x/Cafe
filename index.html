A little about us

Maple & Bean began with one espresso machine and a very large oven. Today it is a neighbourhood café where nobody rushes you out.

We roast in small batches, bake every morning and keep the menu short so everything on it is done well. You will find us at 12 Garden Street, a short walk from the park. Come for a flat white, stay for the almond croissant.
