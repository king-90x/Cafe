Before you visit

A few things that make your visit easier.

Parking

Free street parking on Garden Street, plus a small public lot 2 minutes away.

Wi-Fi

Free, fast Wi-Fi for all guests. Ask at the counter for the password.

Charging points

Power sockets at the window tables and along the work counter.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance and a wide aisle to the main seating area.

Outdoor seating

A shaded garden patio, open until sunset in good weather.

Pet policy

Leashed pets are welcome on the patio only.

Celebrations & group visits

Birthdays, farewells, book clubs and small get-togethers feel right at home here. We can reserve our long communal table or the garden patio for your group.
