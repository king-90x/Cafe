Our story

More Than a Coffee Stop.

A real place, run by real people who care about what ends up in your cup.

Read Our Story

How it started

It began with one espresso machine, one very large oven and a simple idea: a café where nobody feels rushed.

Our founder spent years working in busy city offices, always looking for a quiet table and a properly made cup of coffee. When she could not find both in the same place, she decided to build it herself.

The café opened in a small corner space on Garden Street, with a handful of tables and a menu that fit on one page. Neighbours became regulars, regulars became friends, and the space grew with them.

Why “Maple & Bean”?

The maple tree outside our first window gave us the “Maple”. The beans are the reason you came in. Together they sum up how we work: something rooted and local, and something carefully made.

Our coffee philosophy

Three things we will not compromise on.

01

Choose beans for flavour, not volume

We buy single-origin lots from small farms and taste every batch before it reaches the menu.

02

Let the coffee taste like itself

Our roasts are medium and balanced, so you taste chocolate, caramel and fruit, not just bitterness.

03

Prepare every cup with care

Shots are timed, milk is steamed to order and every machine is cleaned and calibrated daily.

From bean to cup

Five simple steps between the farm and your table.

Select

Single-origin beans from small farms.

Roast

Small batches, roasted to a balanced medium.

Grind

Ground fresh for each shot, never ahead.

Brew

Timed, tasted and adjusted by our baristas.

Serve

Brought to your table while it is still hot.

Made fresh, served with care

Our small kitchen runs on a simple rule: make it today, make it properly.

  • Baked every morningBreads, croissants and cakes come out of our own oven before we open.
  • Short menu, better foodWe keep the menu small so every dish gets proper attention.
  • Seasonal, local produceVegetables and fruit come from nearby suppliers when they are in season.
  • Clear labellingEggless and vegan options are marked, and our team can answer allergy questions.
